Questions about Mars rovers

A quick guide to why rovers travel to Mars and what their instruments can tell us.

What would you like to know?

Why does NASA send rovers instead of only orbiters?

Orbiters can map broad regions from above. Rovers can inspect rocks up close, analyze samples and investigate specific locations on the ground.

Have rovers found life on Mars?

NASA has not confirmed that rovers have discovered Martian life. They study ancient environments and search for clues that may help scientists evaluate past habitability.

Why did Spirit and Opportunity have solar panels?

The twin Mars Exploration Rovers used sunlight for electrical power. Dust buildup and seasonal changes could limit the energy available to their instruments and motors.

What makes Curiosity different?

Curiosity carries a larger scientific laboratory, including instruments that examine mineral and chemical evidence of past habitable environments, and uses a radioisotope power system.

Why is Perseverance collecting samples?

Perseverance is collecting and storing carefully selected Martian rock and soil material so those samples could be studied in greater detail if a future return mission is undertaken.
