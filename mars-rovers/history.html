Mission history

Milestones on Mars

Each generation of rovers has helped researchers ask more detailed questions about the Red Planet.

Launch and landing timeline

  1. 2003–2004

    Spirit and Opportunity

    Spirit: launched June 10, 2003 and landed January 3, 2004. Opportunity: launched July 7, 2003 and landed January 24, 2004. These solar-powered twin rovers looked for evidence of past water. Spirit documented indications of a wetter Martian past. Opportunity found evidence of environments that could once have supported microbial life and operated for almost 15 years.

    Technical design: six wheels, panoramic cameras, rock-analysis tools and solar panels. NASA source

  2. 2011–2012

    Curiosity

    Launched November 26, 2011 and landed August 6, 2012 in Gale Crater. Its mission is to investigate whether Mars ever had conditions suitable for microbial life. Curiosity's instruments found chemical and mineral evidence of formerly habitable environments.

    Technical design: car-sized rover, onboard laboratory instruments, drill, robotic arm and radioisotope power. NASA source

  3. 2020–2021

    Perseverance

    Launched July 30, 2020 and landed February 18, 2021 in Jezero Crater. Its goals include searching for signs of ancient microbial life, examining geology and collecting rock and soil samples for possible future retrieval.

    Technical design: autonomous navigation, advanced imaging, a sampling drill and sealed sample tubes. NASA source
